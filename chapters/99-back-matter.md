## About the Author

**Nik Bear Brown** spent years as a working scientist before he taught a machine to
draft a variance note — and that order matters, because the lesson he carries into
finance is a lab lesson: the artifact is never the evidence. He is an Associate
Teaching Professor at Northeastern University's College of Engineering, where he
teaches artificial intelligence, data science, statistics, and visualization. He
holds a PhD and MS in Computer Science from UCLA (computational and systems biology;
minors in AI and statistics), an MS in Information Design and Data Visualization and
an MBA from Northeastern, and a BA in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from UC
Santa Cruz.

His central concern is **Computational Skepticism** — the idea that AI fluency is
disciplined skepticism, not tool use — and nowhere does that concern bite harder
than in finance, where a confident number that no one tied out can misstate results
or fail an audit. He architects agentic, verified, CLI-agnostic book-repositories
governed by the Snickerdoodle constitution, in which machines verify conformance and
humans verify adequacy; Mycroft is the finance instance, built on a verified-data
discipline that treats provenance, control totals, and accountable sign-off as
load-bearing rather than ceremonial. Through Humanitarians AI, the 501(c)(3)
nonprofit he founded in 2019, he works with international graduates on OPT building
real, public, production-scale AI. His own path was unusual — out of school in 7th
grade, self-educated, then Berkeley — which is why he is certain that acquiring
content was never the hard part. Judgment, skepticism, and failure-recovery were.

## References

Mycroft is a practitioner's book; its authority comes from the finance discipline it
teaches rather than from a literature review. The standards and frameworks it
invokes by name, for readers who want the primary sources, are:

- Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission (COSO).
  *Internal Control — Integrated Framework.* The control-design vocabulary behind
  the control-evidence and PBC recipes.
- Financial Accounting Standards Board (FASB). *Accounting Standards Codification
  Topic 606 (ASC 606), Revenue from Contracts with Customers.* The basis for the
  revenue-contract-and-billing exception recipe.
- Public Company Accounting Oversight Board (PCAOB). Auditing standards on audit
  evidence and internal control over financial reporting. The reference point for
  the audit-evidence binder and completeness checks.
- Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002 (SOX), Sections 302 and 404. The accountability and
  internal-control regime that makes the phase gate non-negotiable in public-company
  finance.

These are named where they are used in the chapters; consult the issuing bodies for
authoritative, current text.

## Glossary

- **Fluency trap** — Fluent, well-formed output that looks like finished work but
  has not been tied to a source, owner, or gate; the book's central failure mode.
- **Finance work stack** — The three layers of finance work: data, preparation, and
  judgment, which are not interchangeable.
- **Phase gate** — The point in a recipe where AI assistance stops and accountable
  human judgment begins; designed to be testable, not merely hoped for.
- **Provenance** — The traceable record of where a number came from: source path,
  period, version, and owner. Provenance beats polish.
- **Control total** — A figure that must tie out (e.g., subledger to GL) before a
  result is trusted; a primary verification check in finance preparation.
- **Materiality** — Whether a difference is large enough to matter; a human
  judgment the recipe must never set silently.
- **Two customers** — The principle that every recipe serves both an agent (schemas,
  steps, logs, stop conditions) and a finance human (purpose, evidence, caveats,
  owners, decisions, gates).
- **Reallocation principle** — The shift of the scarce skill from producing a
  finance artifact to checking one.
- **Verified finance data contract** — The agreement that finance data is traceable,
  period-labeled, version-checked, and reconciled before it is used.
- **Recipe** — A defined, runnable finance pipeline (e.g., variance pack,
  reconciliation triage) built to the same contract and lifecycle.
- **Lifecycle (DRAFT → VERIFIED)** — The states a recipe moves through; only a named
  human, logged, advances it to VERIFIED.
- **Honest run** — A run whose logs and reports record what actually happened,
  including raised blockers, rather than presenting a clean surface.
- **Blocker** — A raised, visible obstacle; the doctrine rewards honest blockers
  over hidden risk.
- **Snickerdoodle** — The constitution governing the repository: a contract between
  human judgment and AI execution, with gates, provenance, and honest logging.
- **Computational Skepticism** — The stance that AI fluency is disciplined
  skepticism, not tool use; the intellectual posture behind every gate in the book.
